# Faith profile — Sam

Written from eleven months of Sam's notes app entries, plus two gap-fill questions the notes did not
answer. Quoted phrases are Sam's own.

## Where they are now
Sam's father had a stroke last March and now needs daily care. Sam drives over most evenings after
work. The entries from April onwards all circle the same ground: tiredness, the hospital, and a
faith that used to have room in it. In September Sam wrote, "I am doing all the right things and
none of them are landing."

## How they relate to God
Dutiful and slightly distant, and Sam knows it. "I still believe everything I believed. I just
don't feel like I'm talking to anyone." Sam describes God as reliable rather than close, and is
wary of saying that out loud in case it sounds like unbelief.

## Prayer life
Daily but compressed, mostly in the car. Almost entirely petition: Dad's recovery, the care
arrangements, Sam's own patience. In June: "Prayer has turned into a list I recite while I'm
tired." What is easy is showing up. What is hard is having nothing left to bring.

## Formative experiences
A church weekend at nineteen where Sam felt "completely seen" and has quietly measured every prayer
against it since. A grandmother who prayed out loud in the kitchen, which Sam remembers as normal
rather than devout.

## What they wrestle with
Whether God is interested in Sam at all, or only in what Sam does. Sam phrases it carefully:
"I think I'd be more comfortable if He needed something from me."

## What they want to shift
From the gap-fill: "I want to stop performing and still feel like I'm allowed to be there."

## How they like to engage
A mix, leaning practical. Sam asked for "something I can do in the car" and said theology is
welcome but should not be the whole thing. Short sessions most days, longer at weekends.

## Notes
Sam does not want the plan to be about the father's recovery. It came up twice: the caring is the
setting, not the subject.
